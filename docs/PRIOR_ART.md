# Prior-art check

| Product | What it does | How Sakshi differs |
|---|---|---|
| [Fixo](https://getfixo.app/) | Converts WhatsApp text and Hindi/Hinglish voice notes into assigned tasks. | Sakshi reconciles independent commercial evidence and blocks unsafe payment recommendations. |
| [Detrack](https://www.detrack.com/construction-and-building/) | Construction delivery management with proof of delivery and tracking. | Sakshi detects contradictions between a challan and a code-mixed on-site voice report before payment review. |
| [DocuClipper](https://www.docuclipper.com/features/invoice-ocr/) | Extracts and validates invoice data for accounts-payable workflows. | Sakshi adds a source-aware, multimodal safety boundary for delivery disputes rather than invoice extraction alone. |

Sakshi's novelty is the evidence-reconciliation workflow: independent sources -> provenance -> deterministic hold policy -> evidence-specific human action.
